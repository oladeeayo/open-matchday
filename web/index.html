    How the predictions work

    Each match gets independent expected goals (xG) for home and away from league average rates × attack strength × opponent defense strength, with venue splits and a shrinkage prior so early-season samples do not explode.

    Form (last ~5 matches) nudges xG. Position context adjusts the baseline: two bottom-side teams that do not score dampen the total even when both have been conceding; two scoring sides or an even-strength clash lift it slightly; a clear mismatch damps the underdog's attack.

    Probabilities come from the Poisson distribution on total goals (Over 1.5 / 2.5 / 3.5), independent team Poissons (BTTS, Home 2+, Away 2+), lightly blended with each side's empirical rates and the league base rate. The Best tip column always shows the strongest market for each match. Pick a focus market to highlight that column and drive the Tips only filter. Figures are informational, not betting advice.
