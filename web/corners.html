How the corner predictions work

Each team's home and away corner rates (corners earned and conceded per match) are computed from finished results in the open-matchday CSV data, with a shrinkage prior (k=4) so early-season samples do not over-react.

Expected total corners for a fixture = home attacking corner rate × away defensive corner rate / league average away corners + the symmetric away calculation. A Poisson distribution converts the expected total into P(over 7.5) and P(over 8.5).

Corners arrive in clusters (a team taking a corner often leads to another shortly after), so the variance is higher than a plain Poisson assumes. We partially compensate by blending with each league's empirical over-line rate weighted by team sample size. Academic work (Yip et al., 2021) models the clustering explicitly with a compound Poisson; the multiplicative + empirical blend is the standard practical approach.

Only the top-10 leagues by UEFA association coefficient are shown, and only fixtures without a recorded result yet (i.e. upcoming). Probabilities are informational, not betting advice.
