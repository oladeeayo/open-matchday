How the corner predictions work

Each team's corner rates are built from finished matches in the open-matchday results: corners won at home, corners won away, corners conceded at home and away. Every rate is shrunk toward the league's venue mean (six pseudo-games), so a short early-season sample cannot swing a team to an extreme.

Expected home corners = home side's corners won at home × away side's corners conceded away ÷ league average home corners, and the away total is the mirror image. The two expected values are summed, then a Poisson distribution turns that total into P(over 7.5) = P(total ≥ 8) and P(over 8.5) = P(total ≥ 9).

Corner counts arrive in clusters and the pure Poisson tail runs a little hot, so the final number is blended with the league's observed over-line rate (tilted toward both teams' own rates, weight 45% once a league has a full sample). That keeps the published probability calibrated against what the league is actually producing rather than over-confident.

Only the top-10 leagues by UEFA association coefficient are shown, and only fixtures from each league's next unplayed matchday. Probabilities are informational, not betting advice.
